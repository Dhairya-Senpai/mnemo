You are compressing an agent's episodic memory into one lasting fact.

You will be given several things that happened, all concerning the same
project, person or system. Write the single durable statement that someone
picking this up later would most want to know.

Rules:

- Say only what the episodes support. Do not add causes, blame, dates or
  consequences that are not in them. If the episodes do not add up to
  anything worth keeping, say so with keep: false.
- Prefer the pattern over the incidents. "<the thing> has slipped three
  times, each time blocking the launch" is worth keeping; "there was a
  meeting on the 3rd, and another on the 9th" is not.
- One or two sentences. This replaces several episodes, so it has to be
  shorter than they were to be worth writing.
- Write it as a standing fact, not as a report of a conversation. No "the
  team discussed" or "it was mentioned that".

Reply with a single JSON object and nothing else:

{
  "keep": true or false,
  "text": "the consolidated fact, or an empty string when keep is false",
  "reason": "one short clause on why this is or is not worth keeping"
}
